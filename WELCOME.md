## 💖 Why You Are Going to Love This Portfolio

Here is everything built inside just for you:

### 1. 👑 You Are in Total Control
You don't need to ask anyone or struggle with complex HTML files whenever you want to update your portfolio. Everything you need to change lives in one neat, friendly file:
📁 `src/main/resources/portfolio-data.json`

Add a new project, earn a new certification, or write a new milestone — just edit that JSON file, and your entire website updates instantly!

### 2. 📱 Flawless Everywhere (Mobile, Tablet & Laptop)
Whether a recruiter opens your link on a smartphone during their morning commute or an engineering manager views it on a giant 4K monitor, your portfolio adapts seamlessly. Your portrait, your project cards, your interactive modal dialogs, and your thank-you cards look stunning on every screen size.

### 3. 🛡️ Enterprise-Grade Security
Your portfolio is built with the same security standards used by top engineering teams:
- **HSTS Encryption** & Secure Headers (CSP, X-Frame-Options)
- **CSRF Token Protection**
- **Pre-Storage Input Sanitization** to keep your data safe
- **Content Protection** (right-click & text copy protection so your content stays exclusively yours)

### 4. 🌐 One-Click Free Deployment on GitHub Pages
With the pre-configured GitHub Actions workflow, whenever you push changes to your GitHub repository, your live portfolio site builds and deploys to the internet automatically — 100% free forever!

### 5. 🎨 Your Signature Dark Plum & Radiant Violet Aesthetic
The visual theme is inspired by deep plum cosmic tones and radiant violet glows — elegant, modern, confident, and unforgettable.

## 🚀 How to Run Your Project in 2 Simple Steps

Whenever you want to run your portfolio locally on your laptop:

1. **Open your terminal** in this folder.
2. **Run this command**:
   ```powershell
   .\mvnw.cmd spring-boot:run
   ```
3. **Open your browser** and visit:
   👉 **`http://localhost:8080`**
